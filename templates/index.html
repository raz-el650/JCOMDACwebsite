About Us

Jesus Christ Our Mighty Deliverer and Anointing Church is a Christ-centered ministry devoted to glorifying God and making disciples through the power of His Word and the guidance of the Holy Spirit. Founded upon the truth of the Holy Scriptures, our church exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, lead people into a personal relationship with Him, and help believers grow in spiritual maturity and godly character.

We believe that Jesus Christ is the Savior, Deliverer, Healer, and King. Through heartfelt worship, fervent prayer, biblical teaching, and genuine Christian fellowship, we seek to create an environment where people can encounter the presence of God, experience His transforming power, and find hope for every season of life.

Our mission is to share the message of salvation, encourage spiritual growth, strengthen families, and reach our community with the love and compassion of Christ. We are committed to raising disciples who will live according to God's Word, serve faithfully in His kingdom, and become a light to the world. We believe that every believer has a purpose given by God and is called to make an impact for His glory.

At Jesus Christ Our Mighty Deliverer and Anointing Church, we embrace people from all walks of life. Whether you are seeking God for the first time, returning to your faith, or looking for a church family where you can grow, you are welcome here. We desire to be a place where hearts are restored, lives are transformed, and faith is strengthened through the power of Jesus Christ.

As a community of believers, we stand firm in our commitment to worship, prayer, discipleship, evangelism, and service. Together, we pursue God's purpose, celebrate His faithfulness, and look forward to the fulfillment of His promises. We invite you to join us as we grow in faith, walk in His anointing, and experience the abundant life that is found in Christ alone.
